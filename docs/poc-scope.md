# Scope of the sample

A team can exercise a fictional expense policy without a financial account. The reviewer chooses a structured payment destination at entry. **Crypto wallet** is prohibited for this team's expenses and automatically receives a final decline. The policy does not classify free text or investigate a real transaction.

The local desk is single-user, carries no money and has no authentication. Use one API worker on loopback with an Android debug build connected through ADB. A hosted multi-user expense service would also need authenticated roles, durable records, ownership checks and an audit log appropriate to its purpose.
